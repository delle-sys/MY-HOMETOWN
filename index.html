Historical Significance and Natural Splendor

WHERE SACRED HISTORY MEETS UNSPOILED COASTAL BEAUTY

Limasawa is an island municipality in Southern Leyte, world-renowned as the site of the first Catholic Mass in the Philippines, celebrated on Easter Sunday, March 31, 1521, by Ferdinand Magellan and Rajah Kolambu. Beyond its monument-filled heritage, Limasawa offers a tranquil paradise: rugged rock formations, serene lagoons, panoramic hilltop views, and thriving marine sanctuaries perfect for eco-tourists and adventurers alike.
